## Third-round review

**(a) Pre-specification scoping — one undisclosed deviation.**
Section 3.5 states that split rules, pass marks and the highway decision rule were "written down before the results they govern" and that any exception is logged as a dated, numbered amendment "written before the data it concerns" (ll. 215–222). The dropped 20 m restriction is handled this way (amendment 4, l. 530). The decision at ll. 533–539, however, is not: the protocol specified dividing highway tree counts by the measured detection rate (0.588), but the authors instead report "densities as detected" after observing that the rate differs inside vs. outside the 2–20 m window. This reasoning could only have been produced after running the detector on the test photos and inspecting the mismatch, yet it is not flagged as an amendment the way the 20 m change is. As written, this is a post-hoc protocol deviation presented with the same "fixed in advance" framing the paper otherwise insists on (l. 77, l. 405). Please either show this was logged as a dated amendment or concede it is exploratory, and revise the "decided before the data" claim in Section 3.5 accordingly.

**(b) Evaluator claims vs. table footnote — direct contradiction.**
Section 5.1 states: "Table 3 compares detectors … with every model scored by the same COCO evaluator (Lin et al., 2014)" (ll. 412–413). Table 3's own footnote *a* says the opposite for the top-ranked model: Faster R-CNN is "Scored with the same COCO evaluator code inside its training job … **rather than by the shared script** on exported predictions." These two statements cannot both be true. This matters because Faster R-CNN (0.652 AP50) sets the upper end of the headline range quoted in the abstract ("0.61–0.65 AP50"); if its score comes from a non-identical evaluation pipeline, the cross-architecture comparison that the range is meant to support is compromised. Fix the body sentence to say "all but one model" (or re-score Faster R-CNN through the shared script) before this goes further.

**(c) Abstract vs. body numbers — checked, consistent.**
I re-verified every quantitative abstract claim against the body: AP50 0.61–0.65 (Table 3, 0.613–0.652); 23 vs. 76 px (ll. 431–433: 23.0 vs. 75.6); AP@4 m 0.640 [0.568–0.690], 0.565 strict (ll. 447–450); median 2.2 m (l. 450: 2.16 m); depth network 0.012 → 0.622 (ll. 478–481); 49% / 17% (ll. 496–497: 49.4%/17.1%); 2.3 m / 1.4 m (ll. 487–489: 2.34/1.42 m); 59% found (ll. 524–526: 58.8%); ρ = 0.48 (l. 545). No mismatches found.

**(d) Remaining major issue.** Beyond (a)/(b), I see no new fatal defect; the previously-disclosed limitations (Pasadena-only validation, flat-ground bias, single annotator, public-trees-only scope, imagery date mismatch) are stated openly and not contradicted elsewhere.

**Verdict: Major revision required** — contingent on (1) resolving the Faster R-CNN evaluator contradiction in Table 3/Section 5.1, and (2) either documenting the highway density-correction decision as a proper dated amendment or explicitly relabeling it exploratory.
