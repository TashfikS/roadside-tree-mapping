## Decision
- **Submit to Urban Forestry & Urban Greening first.** It has by far the most closely related papers, and the
  paper's final product is a planting decision, which is its core scope.
- **If rejected, Computers, Environment and Urban Systems**, then **JAG**. The manuscript is written so that only the
  first paragraph of the Introduction, the Implications section and the cover letter change between these three.
- **ISPRS JPRS only if Test P (trunk point vs box bottom on Pasadena map scores) shows a clear gain.**
- Landscape and Urban Planning, Sustainable Cities and Society and EPB are not recommended without new analyses
  (equity, policy, thermal comfort) that the data do not support.

## Alignment with Urban Forestry & Urban Greening (checked in the manuscript)
- Opens and closes with the urban-forestry question (where to plant, inventory gaps in low-income countries).
- Practice implications for road agencies and tree officers; limitations of plantability stated.
- Method details that matter only to computer-vision readers belong in the appendix / supplementary material.
- Elsevier format: highlights (<= 85 characters each), graphical abstract, CRediT, data availability, declaration of
  generative AI use, line numbers, Harvard (author-year) references.
